Inventory GLB files for the XREAL showroom.

  avante.glb   — sedan class (Hyundai Avante stand-in)
  sonata.glb   — business / sport sedan (Sonata stand-in)
  santa_fe.glb — SUV / crossover (Santa Fe stand-in)

Populate with:  ./gradlew :app:downloadCarModels
(also runs automatically before preBuild). Production should replace these
Khronos / three.js PBR samples with licensed J Motors inventory models.
